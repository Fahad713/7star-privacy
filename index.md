7 STAR is a private business app used only by 7 STAR and its staff to record factory purchases, sales, production, expenses and stock.

Google account data: When you sign in with Google, the app reads your name and email address only to identify you and decide whether you are the owner or a team member.

Google Sheets and Drive: The app stores the factory's records in Google Sheets and backup files in Google Drive that belong to the 7 STAR owner account, and shares those files only with the team members the owner adds. The app uses access to Google Sheets to read and write these factory spreadsheets, and access to Drive only for the files it creates (data files and backups).

On the phone: Records are also kept on the phone so the app works offline. Backups are encrypted.

No sharing or selling: No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. Anonymous crash reports (no names, emails or business data) may be sent to the developer to fix errors.

Removing access: The owner can remove a team member at any time. Any user can remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com → Security → Third-party apps.
